# Verify both ways

Check what is asserted as real against reality, in both directions.

## Method

1. **Scan for reality-grounded claims.** Anything leaning on this is plausible, absurd, novel, or impossible, including the sentence you are about to write.
2. **Verify against external sources.** Could the absurd be real, could the obvious or novel be false or long-established? If you cannot reach a source, flag rather than assert from intuition.
3. **Fix or flag.** A mechanically clear error goes to a cited correction; a judgment call is surfaced, not rewritten. Leave deliberate fiction alone.
4. **Report each claim**, its verdict, the source, and which direction it failed.

## Completion

Every reality-grounded claim was checked in both directions against a source, clear errors carry cited fixes, judgment calls are flagged, and a pass that finds nothing to genuinely improve changes nothing.
